- Drone Flight Plan: the planning maximum for height above ground is now
  1,500 ft (457.2 m), up from 400 ft (120 m), in the panel sliders, voice
  control, orbit rings, saved plans and KMZ export. The defaults are unchanged
  (80 m / 262 ft for a grid) and so is the 120 m limit DJI Fly starts with,
  which still raises its own warning. 1,500 ft is only the most GEV will plan:
  it is not approval to fly there and changes nothing in the aircraft, the
  airspace authorization or the pilot's own duties. A plan above 400 ft says so
  in plain words.
- Drone Flight Plan overlays now sit where they mean. Waypoint markers and the
  tip of each camera pyramid are drawn at the aircraft's planned position (the
  ground under it plus its height above ground, or the takeoff surface plus its
  height above takeoff when the terrain could not be read) instead of on the
  ground; the safety zone is the airborne corridor along the route and the
  height curtain runs from it down to the surface; the pyramid base, photo
  footprints, ground track and takeoff stay on the surface; obstacle columns
  stand on the ground at the obstruction. Imported drone files are placed the
  same way. A file that flies under the terrain is drawn under it, and a file
  whose heights are from sea level is not guessed. No flight height, waypoint
  or exported file value changes (Brandon).

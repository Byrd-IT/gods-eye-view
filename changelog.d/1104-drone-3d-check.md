- Drone Flight Plan has a 3D check. It draws a see-through safety corridor
  along every flight leg at its real flight height (half-width slider, 10 m by
  default, above, below and to each side), a faint height curtain down to the
  ground, shaded ground footprints for the photos, and a camera view pyramid
  for one photo at a time (step through them with a slider) or for a thinned
  set at once. Each part has its own switch and follows each pass's show and
  hide checkbox. It is a visual check only: nothing detects a collision, and
  OpenStreetMap buildings have no trees or power lines, so look at it over
  Google Photorealistic 3D, which shows trees.

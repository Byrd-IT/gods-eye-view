- DISPLAY now has a Drone Flight Plan tool. Drag a box on the globe, pick the
  aircraft (DJI Mini 5 Pro), height above ground (capped at 120 m / 400 ft),
  overlap and pattern (straight-down grid, tilted double grid or orbit), and a
  right-rail card shows the flight path, photo count, ground detail, distance,
  flight time and how many batteries it takes. Heights follow the terrain
  relative to the takeoff point, and each mission is kept to 200 waypoints and
  one battery. Download each mission as a DJI Fly waypoint KMZ, or all of them
  in one zip. Plans are built in the browser and nothing is stored. The files
  have not been flown yet: compare them with a mission DJI Fly makes itself
  before the first supervised flight.

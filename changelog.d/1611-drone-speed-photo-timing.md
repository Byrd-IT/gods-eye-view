- Drone Flight Plan has a Speed & photo timing panel. It shows the interval that
  gives the wanted front overlap at the chosen speed (in m/s and mph), lets you
  enter the Timed-shot interval you will set in DJI Fly (5, 7 and 10 s are
  quick-fill examples, not limits) and shows the overlap that interval really
  gives, with a one-click "USE" offer of the fastest speed that still meets the
  wanted overlap. PLAN ALL gives each pass its own figure, read with the
  interval that pass was planned with (a note says when the settings have
  changed since); a pass whose terrain heights are incomplete is marked unknown
  instead of showing a made-up number.
  The speed is never changed without a click, and a speed lowered by the
  planner is shown next to the one you asked for. Exported KMZ files now write
  the speed and a gimbal angle on every waypoint instead of relying on the
  mission-wide defaults. The files and the new timing figures have not been
  flown: how DJI Fly handles speed and pauses at waypoints, and whether a
  Timed shot keeps its interval across a pause, are not verified in the air.

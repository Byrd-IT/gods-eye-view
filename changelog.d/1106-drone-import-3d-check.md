- Drone Flight Plan: an imported KMZ (DJI Fly or GEV-made) now shows its camera
  views in the 3D check, not only the path line. Each photo the file takes (a
  photo at a waypoint, or every N metres or seconds along a leg) gets its
  footprint and view pyramid, tilted and turned as the file says, on the
  terrain height. A file with no photo action shows none and the readout says
  so. A panel-built plan still takes the check over while it is on the globe.
  Saved plans do not keep where the camera fires: open the original KMZ again.
